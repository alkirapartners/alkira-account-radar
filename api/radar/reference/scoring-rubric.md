# Alkira Fit Scoring Rubric (1-10)

Score how closely a company's network and cloud footprint matches the problems
Alkira solves. A partner uses the score to rank a list of accounts they already
chose to pursue, so the scale leans high: most established enterprises have at
least one real Alkira entry point.

## Scoring Bands

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 9-10 | Textbook fit. Large, multi-region enterprise with known multicloud use and at least one strong trigger: frequent M&A, hundreds of branch, plant or store sites, heavy business-partner connectivity, or regulated segmentation needs. |
| 7-8 | Strong fit. Mid-to-large enterprise with a multi-site or multicloud footprint and a clear entry point. This is the default band for an established enterprise. |
| 5-6 | Plausible fit. A real network, but smaller scale, a single region, or a likely single-cloud posture. Worth a discovery call. |
| 3-4 | Weak fit. Small footprint and little network complexity. |
| 1-2 | Not a fit. A very small business with no meaningful network, a direct Alkira competitor, a networking vendor, or a cloud hyperscaler. |

## Leaning High

- When torn between two scores for an established enterprise, pick the higher one.
- Do not mark a company down for what you do not know. Missing evidence of a
  trigger is not evidence against fit.
- Go below 5 only for a clear, specific reason you can name in a bullet: the
  company is too small, runs from a single site, or sells a competing product.

## What Raises a Score

- Operations across several regions or countries
- Many distributed sites: branches, plants, stores, clinics, terminals
- Known use of more than one cloud provider
- A history of acquisitions, which means networks to integrate and overlapping IP space
- Many business partners, suppliers or customers that need network access
- Regulated industries where segmentation and inspection are mandatory: healthcare, financial services, energy
- Legacy WAN at scale, which makes MPLS replacement a credible entry point
- AI or data workloads spread across environments

## What Lowers a Score

- A small company with one or a few offices
- A business born in a single cloud with no on-prem or branch footprint
- A direct competitor in cloud networking, or a network or cloud provider itself
- A company being absorbed into a much larger parent whose stack will win

## Mapping Reasons to Alkira Entry Points

Where it fits, tie a reason to the entry point it implies:

- Multi-region, multicloud footprint: Hybrid and Multi-Cloud Networking
- Firewall sprawl across clouds or sites: Network and Security Services Consolidation
- Expensive legacy WAN across regions: Backbone-as-a-Service
- Acquisitions or partner connectivity: Extranet-as-a-Service
- Large remote or distributed workforce: Zero Trust Network Access
